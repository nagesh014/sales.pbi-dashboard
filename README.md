# sales.pbi-dashboard

Objective

An interactive dashboard designed to track key sales metrics, analyze performance across multiple dimensions (categories, payment modes, sales types), and evaluate monthly and daily trends.

Key Metrics (KPIs)

  •	Total Sales: 366.62K
  
  •	Quantity Sold: 132 units
  
  •	Total Profit: 34K
  
  •	Profit Margin (%): 10%
  
Key Insights & Features

•	Monthly Sales & Profit Analysis: 
    Combined bar graph highlighting total selling value and profit margins across all 12 months.

•	Daily Sales Trend: 
    Line chart tracking revenue fluctuations over a 30-day period to identify peak sales days.

•	Sales by Category: 
    Treemap visualization showing proportion of sales across categories such as Personal Care, Beverages, Household, Grocery, and Furniture.

•	Sales Breakdown by Product: 
    Top products by performance, including Plastic Mat (21K), Carpet Roll (21K), Rubber Mat (18K), Silk Fabric (18K), and Cotton Fabric (15K).

•	Payment & Distribution Channels: 

o	Payment Mode: 
Cash (50.53% / 185.26K) vs. Online (49.47% / 181.36K).

o	Sale Type: 
Distribution split across Wholesaler (51.68%), Online (33.5%), and Direct Sales (14.82%).

•	Dynamic Slicers/Filters: 
Built-in interactive filtering options for Year (2024, 2025), Month, Sale Type, and Payment Mode.
